**Nerd Fonts** patches developer targeted fonts with a high number of glyphs (icons). Specifically to add a high number of extra glyphs from popular вҖҳiconic fontsвҖҷ such as [Font Awesome](https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome), [Devicons](http://vorillaz.github.io/devicons/), [Octicons](https://github.com/github/octicons), and others.
