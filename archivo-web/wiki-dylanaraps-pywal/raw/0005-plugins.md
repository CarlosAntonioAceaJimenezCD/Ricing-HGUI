Below are plugins that have been written by either myself or another `wal` user. The plugins add support for `wal`'s colorschemes in various different applications.

* [Walcut](https://github.com/plasmoduck/walcut) 
* [Hyper Terminal](https://github.com/dneustadt/hyper-wal)
* [Vim](https://github.com/dylanaraps/wal.vim)
* [Emacs](https://gitlab.com/jjzmajic/ewal)
* [vtop](https://github.com/elock37/wal_vtop)
* [Razer peripherals](https://github.com/LoLei/razer-cli)
* [Zathura](https://github.com/GideonWolfe/Zathura-Pywal)
* [Gnuplot](https://github.com/GideonWolfe/Gnuplot-Pywal)
* [cli-visualizer](https://github.com/aryakaul/cli-visualizer_pywal)
* [Element](https://github.com/acxz/element-wal)
* [Pywalc](https://github.com/png261/pywalc)
